ChordStreak

For iPhone, iPad and Mac. Coming soon to the App Store.

ChordStreak turns piano practice into a game. Play along with scrolling music on a real piano, and ChordStreak listens through your device's microphone, or connects to a MIDI keyboard, to check every note as you play it.

  • Lessons in technique, rhythm, note reading, scales, chords and sight-reading
  • Classical, ragtime and traditional pieces to learn hand by hand, at your own tempo
  • Ear training, theory quizzes and a glossary of score terms
  • Scores and progress tracking, all kept on your device
